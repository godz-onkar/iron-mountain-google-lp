Layout and build notes

The page has one job: get someone who may need shoulder, knee or hip surgery to request a visit with Dr. Ayzenberg, or call. Every button does one of those two things. When you give feedback, name the section number.

Page settings

  • URL: drmarkayzenberg.com/home (the Orthopedic Surgeon ad group and the default page). The shoulder, knee, hip and sports pages are clones with the lines in the copy doc’s table changed.
  • Title: Orthopedic Surgeon in Huntingdon Valley, PA | Dr. Mark Ayzenberg
  • Description: Shoulder, knee and hip surgery with Dr. Mark Ayzenberg in Huntingdon Valley, PA, just outside Philadelphia. Rated 5.0 across 400+ Google reviews.
  • Robots: the preview is noindex. The live page follows the current page’s setting.

Design choices

  • Red means act. The brand red (#8B0507) appears only on Request buttons and links. Call buttons are outlined, so the eye learns the main action.
  • Brand fonts and colors from the live page and the brand guidelines: Poppins (the brand’s own font) for headings and buttons, a calm serif (Newsreader) for reading, warm off-white background, the logo’s slate grey for the ridge line. The logo’s Nexa font is licensed; switch the headings to Nexa in GHL only if Iron Mountain holds the license.
  • Real photos only: Dr. Ayzenberg’s 2024 headshot and the office exterior, both from ironmountainortho.com. The anatomical illustration on today’s page is gone. The site’s ROSA robot photos are left out (that system is mostly for knee replacements, which he doesn’t do).
  • One phone number everywhere, six tap-to-call links. Every Request button opens the survey.

What this preview leaves out

  • Patient videos. The four slots (Bob, Sharon, John, the 6,000-mile patient) are labeled placeholders here. The video files are on the current page; the links are in the vault copy doc, not on this public preview.
  • The survey. Request buttons open a note explaining what happens on the live page. Nothing is submitted.
